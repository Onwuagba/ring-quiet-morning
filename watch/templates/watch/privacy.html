How it is enforced, not just promised

Every incoming event passes an allowlist before it is stored. Only these fields survive: {{ allowed|join:", " }}. Values that look like links or are long are dropped too. Fields such as {{ never|join:", " }} are never kept. The same filter also runs when an event is saved, so no code path can store media by accident.

Consent and control

Owners confirm during setup that the person knows and agrees. Owners can export or delete the household from Settings at any time, and choose how long history is kept.

Honest limits

Quiet Morning is a check-in aid, not a medical or emergency service. Silence can have harmless causes, which is why messages are worded as gentle check-ins and show a confidence score. We have not verified what Ring delivers when video end-to-end encryption is on.
